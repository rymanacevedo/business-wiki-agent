# Commercial Lease Agreement

This lease is made as of April 1, 2025.

- **Landlord:** Harborview Properties LLC, 900 Quay Street, Portside
- **Tenant:** Northwind Repair LLC, a single-member limited liability company (Sam Rivera, Member)

1. **Premises.** Landlord leases to Tenant the premises known as 1480 Harbor Avenue, Suite 210, Portside
   (approx. 850 sq ft, ground floor workshop and office).
2. **Term.** Twenty-four (24) months commencing April 1, 2025 and ending March 31, 2027.
3. **Rent.** $1,450.00 per month, due on the 1st. Late after the 5th ($75 fee).
4. **Security deposit.** $2,900.00, held by Landlord.
5. **Renewal and notice.** This lease renews for one further 12-month term on the same
   terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90)
   days before the end of the term.
6. **Use.** Appliance repair workshop, parts storage, customer drop-off. No vehicle repair.
7. **Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of not
   less than $1,000,000 per occurrence naming Landlord as additional insured.

Signed March 18, 2025.

/s/ Dana Okafor, Manager, Harborview Properties LLC
/s/ Sam Rivera, Member, Northwind Repair LLC
