No threshold is chosen until every judgment is labelled: the judges' `.atLeast(0.7)` in `evals/query/questions.eval.ts` is unchanged.

## Method

The Owner's hand labels in [`docs/evals/runs/before/calibration.yaml`](runs/before/calibration.yaml) are joined by eval id to the judge scores of the `before` run's calibration-tagged Query rows (`runs/before/query.json`). A judge score counts as a pass when it is at least the cut. Agreement is the share of labelled pairs where judge and Owner agree; Cohen's kappa corrects it for chance (n/a when both sides are constant). A row with no score from a judge is left out of that judge's pairs. The judge's boolean score is the judge model's self-reported probability, not a token-level estimate.

To calibrate: set `faithful` and `cited` to `true` or `false` on every entry of `docs/evals/runs/before/calibration.yaml`, run `bun run eval:calibration before`, and copy the chosen thresholds into the judges' `.atLeast`.
