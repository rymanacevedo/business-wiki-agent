# Annual report

- **What**: File annual report with the Secretary of State by the last day of the month in which the company was formed, per the Articles of Organization (src: 2024-10-09-articles-of-organization).

- **How computed**: The Articles of Organization state the company was filed and effective on 2024-10-09. The rule says the report is due by October 31 each year, beginning the year after formation. The next due date on or after the Demo Date (2026-10-17) is 2026-10-31 (src: 2024-10-09-articles-of-organization).

- **Record**: [Articles of Organization](../summaries/2024-10-09-articles-of-organization.md)
