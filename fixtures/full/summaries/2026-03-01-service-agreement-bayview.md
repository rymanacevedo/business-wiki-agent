Appliance Service Agreement between Bayview Residential Management and Northwind Repair LLC (src: 2026-03-01-service-agreement-bayview).

- **Client**: Bayview Residential Management, 310 Beacon Street, Portside (src: 2026-03-01-service-agreement-bayview)

- **Labor rate**: $85.00 per hour (src: 2026-03-01-service-agreement-bayview).

- **Invoicing and terms**: Contractor invoices monthly; invoices are payable net 30 (src: 2026-03-01-service-agreement-bayview).

## Term and renewal

- **Term**: 2026-03-01 to 2027-02-28 (src: 2026-03-01-service-agreement-bayview).
- **Renewal**: automatically renews for successive one-year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term (src: 2026-03-01-service-agreement-bayview).

## Insurance and tax forms

- **Insurance requirement**: Contractor must keep commercial general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and provide the Client a certificate of insurance naming the Client as certificate holder (src: 2026-03-01-service-agreement-bayview).
- **Tax forms**: Contractor provides a completed Form W-9 before the first payment (src: 2026-03-01-service-agreement-bayview).
