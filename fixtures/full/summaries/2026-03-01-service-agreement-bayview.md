# Appliance Service Agreement

- **Parties**: Bayview Residential Management (Client) and Northwind Repair LLC (Contractor) (src: 2026-03-01-service-agreement-bayview).
- **Document date**: March 1, 2026 (src: 2026-03-01-service-agreement-bayview).
- **Services**: Contractor diagnoses and repairs appliances in the residential units the Client manages, on request by the Client's property manager (src: 2026-03-01-service-agreement-bayview).
- **Rates and payment**: $85.00 per hour labor; parts at cost plus 10%; Contractor invoices monthly; invoices are payable net 30 (src: 2026-03-01-service-agreement-bayview).
- **Term**: Begins 2026-03-01 and ends 2027-02-28; renews automatically for successive one-year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term (src: 2026-03-01-service-agreement-bayview).
- **Insurance**: Contractor must keep commercial general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and give Client a certificate of insurance naming Client as certificate holder (src: 2026-03-01-service-agreement-bayview).
- **Tax forms**: Contractor provides a completed Form W-9 before the first payment (src: 2026-03-01-service-agreement-bayview).
- **Termination**: Either party may end this agreement on 30 days' written notice (src: 2026-03-01-service-agreement-bayview).

**Signatures**: Property Manager Priya Nair (Bayview); Sam Rivera, Member, Northwind Repair LLC (src: 2026-03-01-service-agreement-bayview).
